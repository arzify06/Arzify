When you need extra support in your editing process.

You don't always need to outsource the whole edit. Sometimes you just need an expert eye.

Mentoring can take the form of an in-depth film review or one-to-one support via Zoom. The aim is always the same: help you come away with an edit you love.

⌁

Film Review

Arzify reviews your film and supplies practical ways to improve the story, pacing, feel and look. It isn't a pile of criticism — what works is highlighted too.

↗

One-to-One Support

Talk through your editing process, goals and references via Zoom. Sessions can be booked when you need hands-on guidance.

There is never pressure to use another Arzify service later. Mentoring exists to support your creative control.
