About Arzify

Video editor. Storyteller. Secret weapon.

A thoughtful editing partner for people who care about their footage and want their finished films to feel as good as they looked in their heads.

Editing is where meaning takes shape.

Arzu Dobariya brings more than a two of editing experience to projects across drama, documentary, commercial, corporate and wedding filmmaking.

The approach is collaborative and bespoke. Every project has its own rhythm, references and audience. Arzify adapts to your workflow rather than forcing you into someone else's.

After helping shape more than 1500 wedding stories, the focus remains the same: protect the emotion, sharpen the narrative and create an edit you are proud to put your name on.
