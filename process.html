Process

You need a video editor… now what?

From first enquiry to finished video edit, here's what to expect when you trust Arzify with your footage and project.

A bespoke process for your workflow.

Whether you need a freelance editor for a short film, commercial, wedding video or brand project, the broad stages are similar. The details change to fit your rhythm.

If you outsource editing because you don't enjoy it, don't have the time or need another pair of eyes, reference material helps Arzify adapt the edit to your established style.

1

Get in touch

Tell Arzify about your project, needs, references, workflow and ideal outcome. A tricky job can become the start of a longer editing partnership.

2

Finalise the details

Agree the scope and sign an agreement outlining the project so both sides have clarity.

3

Send the footage

Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer or hard drives can be used depending on your setup.

4

The video editing process

Arzify dives into the footage while you provide feedback and request changes or adjustments. The goal is a polished final edit.

5

Export

Once you're satisfied, the final video is exported in the formats you require for clients, online publishing, social media or further finishing.
