FAQ

How much footage can I submit?

That depends on how much you have and the final duration. Arzify can work with small or large collections of material.

What if I don't like the result?

One round of revisions is included in the quoted price. Additional changes can be handled for a small fee.

How do I record a voiceover?

Most smartphones include a voice recorder, and you can also record directly with a phone camera.
